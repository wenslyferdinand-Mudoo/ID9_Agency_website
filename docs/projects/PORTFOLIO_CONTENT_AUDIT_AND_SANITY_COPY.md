# Portfolio editorial audit — Impact Digital 9 Agency

**Purpose:** prepare accurate, consistent project entries for the agency portfolio and Sanity CMS.  
**Editorial rule:** publish confirmed work; keep missing facts unpublished until verified.  
**Scope:** information currently supplied by the agency owner and project records available in the repository. This document is a content draft, not a claim that the Sanity records have already been updated.

## Editorial standard

Each project page should distinguish:
1. **Context** — who the client is and what the project concerns.
2. **Scope** — the actual work delivered by Impact Digital 9 Agency.
3. **Creative approach** — only the rationale supported by the original brief or approved by the client.
4. **Deliverables** — concrete assets, formats and versions.
5. **Outcome** — observable delivery only; no invented commercial impact.
6. **Evidence** — approved artwork, mockups, publication rights, year and client approval.

Do not fill KPIs, testimonials, business challenges, strategic rationale, tools or project dates by inference.
