### Project title
CDMG Haïti — Brand Identity & Visual Communication

### Sector

### Short summary
A branding and visual communication project for CDMG Haïti, featuring logo design, promotional flyers and the development of a consistent visual identity for a dance and cultural institution.

### Context
The Centre de Danse Markenley George (CDMG Haïti) is an artistic and cultural institution dedicated to dance and artistic expression.

Impact Digital 9 Agency contributed to its visual communication through a creative project focused on brand identity, logo creation and promotional graphic materials. The objective was to establish a recognizable visual presence and provide communication assets suited to the institution's needs.

### 01 — The Challenge
Develop a coherent visual identity and promotional communication assets that represent CDMG Haïti's artistic universe while strengthening visual recognition across its communication channels.

### 02 — Our Approach
Impact Digital 9 Agency used a design-led creative approach, focusing on visual identity development, logo creation and promotional assets aligned with the institution's artistic positioning.

### 03 — The Strategy
The visual communication strategy was guided by three principles: consistency, recognition and adaptability.

The objective was to create professional graphic assets that represent the institution and can support promotional campaigns and digital communication.

### 04 — The Solution
The project delivered a set of visual communication assets, including logo creation, promotional flyers and graphic elements designed to support CDMG Haïti's brand presence.

### Outcome
A distinctive visual identity and a set of promotional graphic assets developed to strengthen CDMG Haïti's visual communication.
